# FH-520 Troubleshooting: Cutting Performance and Belts

## Safety first
This guide covers cutting problems on the FH-520 stump grinder. Always shut off the engine, pull the spark plug wire, and wait for the cutter wheel to stop before you inspect it. If you see smoke, if the wheel wobbles hard, or if the machine throws parts, stop using the FH-520 and contact Fernhollow.

## Slow cutting
Symptoms: the grinder cuts slowly or the engine strains. Likely causes, in order:
1. Worn or dull carbide teeth. Check all 16 teeth.
2. Loose tooth bolts.
3. Loose drive belt that slips.
4. Rocks or dirt in the stump. Dirt dulls teeth fast.
Rotate each tooth to a fresh edge, or replace it.

## Cutter wheel stops under load
Symptoms: the wheel stops when you push into the stump. Likely causes: feeding too deep, a slipping belt, or dull teeth. Take smaller sweeps. Check the belt tension. Check the teeth. Remove rocks from the stump area.

## Belt squeal or smoke from the belt area
A squeal means a loose or glazed belt. Smoke or a burning smell means the belt is slipping hard or the wheel is jammed. Stop the engine at once. Stop using the FH-520 and contact Fernhollow if you see smoke or flame. If there is no smoke, let the machine cool, then check the belt tension and the wheel for a jam.

## Broken or missing teeth
Teeth can break on rocks or hit metal. A missing tooth makes the wheel unbalanced and can cause a wobble. Do not run the grinder with a missing tooth. Replace it with a new carbide tooth from the parts page. Replace damaged teeth in sets.

## Wheel wobble
A wobbling wheel can mean a bent wheel, a missing tooth, loose tooth bolts, or a bad bearing. Stop the engine. Check the teeth and bolts. If the wheel wobbles with all teeth in place and tight, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.

## Tooth inspection and belt tension steps
1. Shut off the engine and pull the spark plug wire.
2. Remove the wheel cover.
3. Look at each tooth for chips, cracks, or rounded tips.
4. Tighten loose tooth bolts. Rotate a worn tooth to a fresh edge.
5. Check the belt for slack. Adjust the tension as the manual shows.
6. Spin the wheel by hand. It should turn freely.
Replace the belt if it is cracked or glazed. Keep the model and serial number ready when you contact the Service desk.
