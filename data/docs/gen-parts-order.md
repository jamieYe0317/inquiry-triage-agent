# Buying Parts and Accessories

## What you can buy
You can buy wear parts and accessories for Fernhollow machines. These include:
- chipper blades for the FH-340 wood chipper
- carbide teeth for the FH-520 stump grinder
- drive belts
- nozzles for the FH-P90 pressure washer
- wheel kits
- machine covers
This page lists no prices. The website shows current prices.

## How to find the right part
1. Open the owner's manual for your machine.
2. Find the parts list and the drawing.
3. Read the part number.
4. Check that the part fits your model.
The manuals are for the FH-210 log splitter, the FH-340 wood chipper, the FH-520 stump grinder, the FH-P90 pressure washer, and the FH-G7 generator. If you cannot find the part number, send a photo and the serial number to the Service desk.

## Where to order
Order through the Fernhollow website at example.com with the part number. You can also order from an authorized dealer. Dealers can help you check fit and may stock common wear parts. For help placing an order, call 555-0100 during office hours.

## Wear parts and the warranty
Blades, carbide teeth, belts, and pump seals are wear parts. They have a 90-day warranty period. A worn wear part is normal wear. It is not a defect. If a part failed early, send photos and the serial number to the Service desk. A Fernhollow staff member reviews the case and decides.

## Safety
Use only Fernhollow parts that fit your model. Always shut off the engine and pull the spark plug wire before you change a blade, a tooth, or a belt. If a part fails and a piece is thrown, or if there is fire, smoke, or injury, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.
