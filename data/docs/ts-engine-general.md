# Small Engine Troubleshooting for All Fernhollow Machines

## Scope and safety
This guide covers gas engine problems on all five Fernhollow machines: the FH-210 log splitter, the FH-340 wood chipper, the FH-520 stump grinder, the FH-P90 pressure washer, and the FH-G7 generator. Turn off the engine and let it cool before you work on it. Do not smoke or use flame near fuel. If you see fuel leaking, smoke, or fire, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.

## Engine will not start or is hard to start
Check in order:
1. Fuel level and fuel valve.
2. Choke. Use full choke for a cold engine. Open it as the engine warms.
3. Oil level. Low oil can stop the engine from starting.
4. Spark plug. A fouled plug is a common cause. Clean or replace it.
5. Fuel age. Old fuel is the main cause of a no-start.
6. Air filter. A dirty filter chokes the engine.

## Will not start after storage
Fuel that sits for months goes stale. Fuel that has ethanol absorbs water and leaves varnish. Varnish blocks the carburetor. Drain old fuel. Add fresh fuel. Clean or replace the spark plug. If the engine still will not start, the carburetor may need cleaning. Next time, use fuel stabilizer for storage longer than 30 days.

## Flooded engine
Too much choke or too many pulls can flood the engine. Set the choke open. Remove the spark plug and let it dry. Pull the cord a few times with the plug out. Put the plug back and try again.

## Engine runs rough, surges, or smokes
- Runs rough or surges: stale fuel, dirty carburetor, dirty air filter, or a fouled plug.
- White smoke: oil in the cylinder from tilting the machine, or too much oil. Run the engine for a few minutes.
- Blue smoke: oil is burning. Check the oil level. If blue smoke continues, contact the Service desk.
- Black smoke: too rich a mix, usually a dirty air filter or choke left on.

## What voids the warranty
The Fernhollow engines run on fresh, clean gasoline. A wrong fuel mix, stale fuel, or no oil is not a defect. Damage from these causes is not covered by the warranty. A Fernhollow staff member reviews each case and decides.

## When to contact the Service desk
Contact the Service desk if the engine still fails after these checks. Send the model, serial number, hours of use, fuel age, and photos.
