# Partner Defect Report Procedure

## Who this procedure is for
This procedure is for Fernhollow dealers and distributors. Use it to report a defect that you see on one or more Fernhollow units. Customers who bought direct should use the returns policy.

## What to include in a defect report
Send the Quality team these items:
1. Model and serial numbers of each unit.
2. Lot number or ship date.
3. A clear description of the failure.
4. Hours of use when the failure happened.
5. Photos or video of the failure.
6. The number of units affected.
7. Whether the units are quarantined, which means set aside and not sold.
A report with these items lets the Quality team start sooner.

## Safety issues
If the defect involves fire, smoke, burns, a fuel leak, electric shock, thrown parts, or injury, treat it as a safety case. Tell the user of the machine to stop using it. Send the report to the Quality team at once and mark it urgent. See the Safety Escalation Rule.

## Quarantine
If you see the same defect on several units, set those units aside. Do not sell them. Do not repair them in a way that hides the failure. Tell the Quality team the count and the location of the units.

## What the Quality team does
The Quality team investigates each report. It may ask you to send one unit back to Fernhollow for inspection. It may ask for more photos or service records. After the investigation, a Fernhollow staff member decides on credits or replacements. No outcome is promised during the investigation.

## How to send the report
Email quality@example.com or call 555-0160. Give your partner account name and contact. You will receive a case number. Use the case number in all later messages.
