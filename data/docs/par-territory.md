# Partner Territory FAQ

## How territories work
Fernhollow organizes partners by territory. A regional distributor covers a set of states. A dealer sells in a local area. A territory tells which partner serves which area.

## Exclusivity
Dealers are not exclusive by default. This means another Fernhollow dealer can serve the same area. Exclusivity is negotiated case by case by the Partner team. It is never promised in email. A Fernhollow staff member on the Partner team reviews each request. If you want exclusive rights for your area, ask in your application. The decision comes only through a signed agreement.

## Overlapping dealers
When two dealers serve the same area, the Partner team looks at the case. It may ask both dealers about service capability, customer location, and sales history. The team decides how to handle it. It may keep both dealers, adjust the area, or take another step. Please send the details to the Partner team and do not contact the other dealer to settle it.

## Inquiries from outside the United States
Fernhollow sells in the United States. Inquiries from outside the United States, such as Canada or Mexico, go to the Partner team for a case-by-case review. The team decides if a partnership is possible. Do not assume Fernhollow can ship or serve outside the United States. Contact partners@example.com with your business details.

## How to ask about your territory
Email partners@example.com or call 555-0150. Give your business name, your location, and the area you want to serve. The Partner team replies after review. Staff cannot confirm territory rights over email or phone. The signed agreement states them.
