# FH-520 Stump Grinder Owner's Manual

## Specifications
The FH-520 stump grinder has a 14 hp gas engine. It has a 12-inch cutter wheel with 16 carbide teeth. A drive belt moves the cutter wheel. Keep the serial number from the frame label for service and warranty questions.

## Setup
Set up the FH-520 stump grinder on firm ground. Attach the handles, the wheels, and the debris shield as shown in the parts list. Fill the engine with oil. Add fresh gasoline. Check that the tooth bolts are tight. Check the belt tension. Do not use the machine without the debris shield.

## Safe operation
Call before you dig. Contact your local utility locating service to mark buried lines before you grind. Wear safety glasses, a face shield, hearing protection, heavy boots, and long pants. Keep the debris shield on at all times. Keep bystanders, children, and pets 50 feet away. Rocks and wood chips can be thrown from the cutter wheel. Do not grind near a fence, a wall, or a window. Remove stones and metal from the stump area first. If the FH-520 throws parts, makes smoke, or the wheel wobbles, stop the engine. Stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.

## Tooth rotation and replacement
Check all 16 carbide teeth before each job. A tooth that is dull, chipped, or loose cuts slowly and puts stress on the belt. 
1. Turn off the engine and pull the spark plug wire. Wait for the wheel to stop.
2. Remove the cutter wheel cover.
3. Loosen the tooth bolt. Rotate the tooth to a fresh edge.
4. Tighten the bolt. Replace any tooth with a crack or a broken tip.
5. Check that every tooth is tight and that the wheel spins freely by hand.
Order new carbide teeth from the parts page. Replace damaged teeth in sets for balance.

## Belt tension
Check the drive belt tension every 25 hours. A belt that is loose slips, squeals, and wears out fast. Adjust the belt as shown in the parts list diagram. Replace a belt with cracks or glazing.

## Maintenance schedule and storage
- Check the teeth and bolts before every job.
- Check the belt every 25 hours.
- Change the engine oil every 50 hours.
- Clean the air filter every 25 hours.
For storage, let the engine cool, clean wood chips from the frame, and add fuel stabilizer for storage longer than 30 days. Store the stump grinder in a dry place. For slow cutting or belt noise, see the FH-520 troubleshooting guide.
