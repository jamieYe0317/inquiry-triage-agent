# Replacement Parts Process

## How to ask for a replacement part
If a part is missing or defective, you can ask Fernhollow to replace it. Customers contact Customer care. Dealers and distributors contact the Quality team. Send these items:
1. The part number from the parts list in the manual.
2. The order number or the serial number of the machine.
3. The model, such as FH-520 stump grinder.
4. Photos of the missing area or the defective part.
5. A short description of the problem.

## Finding the part number
Each manual has a parts list with a drawing and part numbers. Find the part in the drawing, and read its number. If you are not sure, send photos and the serial number. Staff can help to find the part.

## Is the part covered?
Customer care or the Quality team confirms if a part is covered before it ships. A part may be covered when it is missing from a new machine or when it failed from a defect. A worn part, such as a dull blade, is usually a wear part and is not a defect. A Fernhollow staff member reviews each request and decides. Do not assume a part is free until staff confirm it in writing.

## Wrong or defective replacement parts
If Fernhollow sends the wrong part, or the replacement part is defective, tell Customer care or the Quality team. Partners should use the Partner Defect Report Procedure for repeat problems. Send the part number, the packing slip, and photos. Keep the wrong part until staff give instructions.

## Safety-related parts
If a part failure caused a fire, smoke, a fuel leak, thrown parts, or injury, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow at once. This is a safety case. See the Safety Escalation Rule.

## Buying parts
If a part is not covered, you can buy it. See the page on buying parts and accessories.
