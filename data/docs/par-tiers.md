# Partner Tiers and Requirements

## The three tiers
The Fernhollow Partner Program has three tiers: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. A partner's tier depends on four things:
- annual sales volume
- the number of trained technicians
- demo units on display
- warranty service capability
A higher tier needs more of each.

## What each tier looks for
- Bronze: a new or small partner. It sells Fernhollow machines and may do basic service.
- Silver: a partner with a larger volume, at least one trained technician, and a demo unit on display.
- Gold: a partner with the highest volume, several trained technicians, demo units on display, and full warranty service capability.
The Partner team sets the exact requirements in the signed partner agreement.

## How to request a tier review
Email partners@example.com. Give your business name, your current tier, your volume, the number of trained technicians, the demo units on display, and your warranty service record. The Partner team reviews the request. A Fernhollow staff member decides if the tier changes. A review does not promise a change.

## Prices and discounts
Price lists and discounts are shared only by the Partner team after an agreement is signed. This page gives no prices and no discount numbers. Staff cannot share prices or discounts in an inquiry reply. Do not ask for them before you sign.

## Training
Partners get product training for the FH-210 log splitter, the FH-340 wood chipper, the FH-520 stump grinder, the FH-P90 pressure washer, and the FH-G7 generator. Trained technicians help a partner reach a higher tier. Ask the Partner team how to book a training session.
