# FH-340 Troubleshooting: Vibration, Belts, and Blades

## Safety first
This guide covers heavy vibration, belt problems, and blade problems on the FH-340 wood chipper. Shut off the engine, pull the spark plug wire, and wait for the drum to stop before you inspect anything. Heavy vibration can mean a loose blade, which can be thrown. If the machine shakes hard, rattles loudly, or throws parts, stop using the FH-340 and contact Fernhollow.

## Heavy vibration or rattling
Likely causes and checks, in order:
1. Loose blade bolts. Check both blades and bolts.
2. Chipped or cracked blades. Look at the edges. A chipped blade makes the drum unbalanced.
3. Damaged drum bearing. Turn the drum by hand. Grinding or roughness means a bad bearing.
4. Loose mounting bolts on the engine or frame.
Do not run the FH-340 with a loose blade or a bad bearing.

## Uneven or stringy chips
Dull blades cause stringy chips. Reverse the blades to use the second edge. If both edges are dull, replace both blades as a pair. Uneven chips can also come from a loose belt that slows the drum.

## Drum does not spin while the engine runs
This usually means the drive belt has slipped, has come off, or has broken. Turn the engine off. Look for a belt that is off the pulley, torn, or glazed. Check the belt tension. If the belt is in place and the drum still does not spin, the drum may be seized by a bearing. Contact the Service desk.

## Belt squeal, burning-rubber smell, or belt slipping
Symptoms: a high squeal when you feed wood, a burning-rubber smell, a belt that slips under load. Likely causes: a loose belt, a worn or glazed belt, or a jam that holds the drum. Shut off the engine. Check for a jam first. Then check the belt tension and the belt surface. Replace a belt that is cracked, frayed, or shiny. If you smell burning or see smoke, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.

## When to stop using the machine
Stop using the FH-340 and contact the Service desk if:
- blades are cracked, or bolts keep coming loose
- the drum bearing is rough or noisy
- the belt burns or smokes
- the machine throws parts
Have the model, the serial number, the hours of use, and photos ready. A Fernhollow staff member reviews each case and decides on parts or repair.
