# Freight Damage and Shipment Discrepancy Claims

## Who this applies to
This process is for dealers and distributors that receive pallet shipments from Fernhollow. It covers freight damage, wrong items, and short quantities.

## At delivery
Before you sign the delivery receipt, look at the pallets and the boxes. Write any damage or shortage on the delivery receipt. Take photos of the pallet, the wrapping, the damaged boxes, and the damaged units. If you sign without noting damage, it may be harder for Fernhollow to file a carrier claim.

## Report within 5 business days
Report damage, wrong items, or short quantities to the Quality team within 5 business days of delivery. Send:
1. The order number and the delivery date.
2. A copy of the delivery receipt with the notes.
3. Photos of the pallet and the units.
4. The model and the quantity that is damaged, wrong, or short.
5. A short description.

## Keep the packaging
Keep all the packaging, the pallet, and the damaged units until the carrier inspects them. Do not throw anything away. Do not sell damaged units. If a unit shows a fuel leak or hydraulic leak, do not use it and contact Fernhollow.

## Wrong items or short quantities
Compare the packing list with the items you received. Tell the Quality team which items are wrong or missing. Do not return anything until the Quality team gives instructions.

## What the Quality team does
The Quality team files the carrier claim. After review, a Fernhollow staff member decides on replacement stock. No outcome is promised before review. Contact quality@example.com or 555-0160.
