# Fernhollow Limited Warranty Summary

## Coverage periods
The Fernhollow Limited Warranty covers the FH-210 log splitter, the FH-340 wood chipper, the FH-520 stump grinder, the FH-P90 pressure washer, and the FH-G7 generator. The coverage period depends on how you use the machine:
- 2 years for residential use.
- 1 year for commercial or rental use.
- 90 days for wear parts, such as blades, carbide teeth, belts, and pump seals.
The period starts on the date of purchase shown on your proof of purchase.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. For example, a weld that cracks under normal use, a hydraulic cylinder that leaks from a faulty seal, or a part that fails in normal use may be a defect. A Fernhollow staff member reviews each claim to decide if a defect is the cause.

## What the warranty does not cover
The warranty does not cover:
- normal wear of parts, such as dull blades or worn belts
- misuse or use that the manual does not allow
- damage from stale fuel, a wrong fuel mix, or no oil
- freezing damage, such as a cracked pump head on the FH-P90 pressure washer
- damage from lack of maintenance
- damage in shipping that you did not report in time (see the returns policy)
A fuel problem is not a defect.

## How to start a claim
Send these items to Customer care through the web form or the phone line:
1. The model, such as FH-340 wood chipper.
2. The serial number from the label.
3. Proof of purchase, such as a receipt or an order number.
4. Photos of the problem and of the serial label.
5. A short description: what happened, the hours of use, and when it started.
If the problem is a safety issue, such as fire, smoke, or a fuel leak, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow first.

## How claims are decided
A Fernhollow staff member reviews every claim and decides the remedy. The remedy can be a repair, a replacement part, a replacement machine, or no remedy if the cause is not covered. Nothing is approved until a Fernhollow staff member confirms it in writing. Do not assume a claim is approved from a phone call or a chat. The staff member may ask for more photos or for the machine to be sent for inspection.
