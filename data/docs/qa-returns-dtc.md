# Returns and Damaged-on-Arrival Policy for Customers

## Who this policy is for
This policy is for people who bought a Fernhollow machine directly from Fernhollow. If you bought from a dealer, contact the dealer. Dealers and distributors should use the Partner Defect Report Procedure.

## Return window
You may ask to return a new, unused machine within 30 days of delivery. The machine must be in its original box and in its original condition. Used machines go through the warranty process instead.

## Damaged on arrival
If your machine arrived damaged, report it within 7 days of delivery. Take photos of the outer box, the damage on the box, and the damaged machine or part. Keep the box and packing until Customer care replies. Do not use a machine that arrived damaged, especially one that shows a fuel leak or hydraulic leak.

## Missing parts and defective on first use
Report missing parts as soon as you find them. Check the parts list in the manual first. If a machine fails the first time you use it, stop using it and contact Customer care. If there is a fuel leak, smoke, or fire, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow at once.

## What to send
Send Customer care these items:
1. Your order number.
2. The model, such as FH-340 wood chipper.
3. The serial number from the label.
4. Photos of the problem.
5. A short description of what happened.
Use the web form on example.com or call 555-0100 during office hours.

## How cases are decided
Customer care reviews each case. A Fernhollow staff member decides on a repair, a part, an exchange, or a refund. No outcome is promised before the review ends. Staff will confirm the decision in writing. Do not ship the machine back until Customer care gives you return instructions.
