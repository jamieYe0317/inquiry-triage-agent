# FH-G7 Portable Generator Owner's Manual

## Specifications
The FH-G7 portable generator makes 7,000 running watts and 9,000 starting watts. It has a 6-gallon fuel tank and a gas engine. It has a low-oil shutdown sensor that stops the engine when the oil is low. It has two 20A outlets and one 30A outlet. Each circuit has a breaker. Keep the serial number from the frame label for service and warranty questions.

## Carbon monoxide warning
The FH-G7 makes carbon monoxide, a gas that has no smell and can kill. Use the generator outdoors only. Put it at least 20 feet from windows, doors, and vents. Never run it in a garage, a basement, a shed, or near an open window. If you feel dizzy, sick, or weak while the generator runs, move to fresh air and get medical help. Stop using the generator and contact Fernhollow.

## Grounding and connection
Ground the generator as the wiring diagram shows. Use heavy-duty outdoor cords that fit the outlet and the load. Do not plug the FH-G7 into a wall outlet in your house. Never backfeed a house without a transfer switch. Backfeeding can send power back to utility lines and injure line workers. Only a licensed electrician should install a transfer switch.

## Fuel and oil
Turn off the engine and let it cool before you add fuel. Do not smoke or use flame near fuel. If fuel leaks from the tank, the line, or the carburetor, or if you see smoke or fire, stop using the FH-G7 and contact Fernhollow. Check the oil before each use. The low-oil shutdown sensor will stop the engine if oil is low, so add oil before you try to restart.

## Maintenance schedule
- Check the oil before each use.
- Change the oil every 100 hours of use.
- Clean or replace the air filter every 50 hours.
- Check the spark plug every 100 hours.
Do not overload the generator. Add the starting watts of motors to your load plan.

## Storage
For storage longer than 30 days, add fuel stabilizer and run the engine for a few minutes, or drain the fuel. Store the FH-G7 in a dry place away from heat. If the generator will not start, see the FH-G7 will-not-start guide. If the outlets give no power, see the FH-G7 outlet guide.
