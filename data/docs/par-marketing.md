# Co-op Marketing and Demo Unit Program

## Co-op marketing
Partners can ask for co-op marketing support. This can cover local ads, fair or trade show booths, and store signage. Fernhollow does not list amounts on this page. The Partner team sets what it can support for each request.

## How to request support
1. Write a short plan. State the event or ad, the dates, the place, and the goal.
2. Add the media or booth details.
3. Send the plan to partners@example.com before you spend money on it.
4. Wait for written approval from the Partner team.
Each request needs a plan. The Partner team approves each request. A Fernhollow staff member reviews each plan and decides. Do not start the work and ask for support later. Support given for a plan is not guaranteed for a later plan.

## Demo unit program
A demo unit is a Fernhollow machine that a partner shows in the store or at an event. Partners can ask for a demo unit, such as an FH-210 log splitter or an FH-P90 pressure washer.

## How to request a demo unit
Send the Partner team your partner name, the model, the place where it will be displayed, and the dates. The Partner team decides if it can send a unit.

## Rules for display and return
- Display the demo unit in a clean, safe place.
- Keep the unit in working order. Do not sell it unless the Partner team agrees in writing.
- Keep the safety labels on the machine.
- Return the unit to Fernhollow when asked, or when the program ends. Fernhollow will give return instructions.
- Report damage to the Partner team at once.
No amounts are listed on this page. The Partner team gives terms in writing.
