# FH-G7 Troubleshooting: No Power at the Outlets

## Safety first
This guide covers an FH-G7 portable generator that runs but gives no power at the outlets. Never open the control panel while the engine runs. If you smell burning or see smoke from the control panel, shut off the engine, unplug everything, and do not run the unit again. Stop using the FH-G7 and contact Fernhollow.

## Engine runs but the outlets give no power
Check in order:
1. Breakers. The FH-G7 has a breaker for each outlet group. Press a tripped breaker back in.
2. Cords and plugs. Test the cord on another device.
3. Loose outlet. If an outlet feels loose or a plug does not hold, stop using that outlet.
4. Engine speed. The engine should run at a steady speed.
5. Loss of residual magnetism. After long storage, the generator may lose the small charge that starts power. Contact the Service desk for the reset steps.
6. Damaged wiring. This needs repair by Fernhollow.

## Breaker trips
A breaker trips when the load is too high or when a device has a fault. The FH-G7 makes 7,000 running watts and 9,000 starting watts. Motors, such as pumps and air conditioners, need extra starting watts. Turn off all devices. Reset the breaker. Plug in loads one at a time, largest first. If the breaker trips with no load, stop using the generator and contact Fernhollow.

## Lights flicker or voltage drops under load
This usually means overload, a long thin extension cord, or an engine that bogs down. Reduce the load. Use a heavy-duty cord that is rated for the load. If the engine surges, see the FH-G7 will-not-start guide.

## Burning smell or smoke from the control panel
This is a safety case. Shut off the engine. Unplug everything. Do not run the generator again. Stop using the FH-G7 and contact Fernhollow at once. Do not try to open the panel yourself.

## What to send
Send the model, the serial number, the hours of use, what was plugged in, and photos of the panel and outlets. A Fernhollow staff member reviews each case and decides on repair.
