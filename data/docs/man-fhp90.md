# FH-P90 Pressure Washer Owner's Manual

## Specifications
The FH-P90 pressure washer is a gas-powered washer. It makes 3,100 PSI at 2.5 GPM. It has an axial pump. It comes with 5 quick-connect nozzles and a 25-foot hose. Keep the serial number from the frame label for service and warranty questions.

## Setup
Set up the FH-P90 pressure washer outdoors on a level surface. Never run it in a closed space, because engine exhaust has carbon monoxide. Add pump oil to the pump. Add engine oil and fresh gasoline to the engine. Connect a garden hose to the water inlet. Turn the water on and squeeze the trigger to purge air before you start the engine. Attach the 25-foot high-pressure hose and the wand. Click a nozzle into the wand.

## Safe use
The spray from the FH-P90 can cut skin. Never point the wand at people, pets, or yourself. Never put your hand or finger over the nozzle. High-pressure injection injury can happen. The spray can push fluid under the skin, and the wound can look small but be serious. If the spray hits your skin, stop the machine and get medical help at once. Then contact Fernhollow. Release the trigger lock when you stop, and always engage the trigger lock when you put the wand down. Do not use a damaged hose. If a hose or fitting bursts or leaks, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.

## Nozzles
The 5 quick-connect nozzles have different spray angles. Use a wide angle for first passes on soft surfaces. Use a narrow angle only on hard surfaces, and keep a safe distance. Pull back the collar on the wand to change a nozzle. Never change a nozzle with the engine running and the trigger pressed.

## Winterizing
Water in the pump can freeze and crack the pump head. This damage is not covered by warranty. Before cold weather, run pump saver through the pump. Disconnect the hoses and drain water from the pump. Store the washer in a place that stays above freezing.

## Maintenance schedule
- Check pump oil before each use.
- Change pump oil after the first 25 hours, then every 100 hours.
- Change engine oil every 50 hours.
- Clean the water inlet filter often.
- Check the high-pressure hose for cracks before each use.
For low pressure or surging, see the FH-P90 pressure and pump troubleshooting guide.
