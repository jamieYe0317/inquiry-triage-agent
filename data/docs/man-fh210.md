# FH-210 Log Splitter Owner's Manual

## Specifications
The FH-210 log splitter is a 22-ton gas-powered log splitter with a hydraulic ram. It has a 6.5 hp gas engine. It splits logs up to 25 inches long. The hydraulic system uses AW-32 hydraulic fluid. Do not use any other fluid in the FH-210. Keep the serial number from the label on the beam. You need it for service and warranty questions.

## Setup
Unpack the FH-210 log splitter on flat, level ground. Attach the wheels and the tow bar as shown in the parts list. Fill the engine with oil before the first start. Check the AW-32 hydraulic fluid level with the dipstick. Add fluid if it is low. Add fresh gasoline to the engine tank. Move the splitter to an open outdoor area before you start it.

## Safe operation
Read this section before each use. Wear safety glasses, gloves, and boots with steel toes. Use the two-hand control. Hold the control lever with one hand and place the log with the other only while the ram is stopped. Keep your hands and feet away from the wedge. Stand clear of the wedge at all times when the ram moves. Never split a knotty log at an angle. A log that is not flat on the beam can shoot out and cause injury. Never let a child or a pet near the FH-210. If a part breaks, a hose bursts, or fluid sprays out, stop the engine. Stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.

## Daily checks
Before each use, check these items:
1. Engine oil level.
2. Hydraulic fluid level.
3. Hoses and fittings for leaks or cracks.
4. Wedge and beam for damage.
5. Fuel level and fuel leaks.
Do not use the splitter if you see a leak. Contact the Fernhollow Service desk.

## Maintenance schedule
- Change the engine oil every 50 hours of use.
- Change the AW-32 hydraulic fluid every 150 hours of use.
- Grease the beam before each season and after each 25 hours of use.
- Check the engine air filter and spark plug at each oil change.
Wear parts such as the hydraulic filter and the wheel bearings may need earlier replacement under heavy use. See the Fernhollow parts page to buy parts.

## Storage
Let the engine cool. Store the FH-210 log splitter in a dry place. For storage longer than 30 days, add fuel stabilizer or drain the fuel tank. Lower the ram fully. Cover the machine. If the splitter will not start after storage, see the small engine troubleshooting guide. If the ram moves slowly or leaks, see the FH-210 hydraulic troubleshooting guide.
