# Shipping and Order Status

## Ship times
Machines that are in stock ship in 2 to 4 business days after we receive your order. If a machine is not in stock, we will tell you after you order. Ship times do not count weekends and holidays. A ship time is an estimate. It is not a guarantee.

## Delivery types
Smaller items ship by parcel. Larger machines, such as the FH-210 log splitter, the FH-340 wood chipper, the FH-520 stump grinder, and the FH-G7 generator, ship by freight. Freight delivery usually comes by truck, and the driver will call before delivery. Curbside delivery means the driver puts the machine at the curb or the end of the truck. You need people and a way to move the machine into your garage or yard.

## How to track an order
Use your order number and the tracking link in the shipping email. You can also call 555-0100 and give your order number. Tracking starts after the carrier scans the package.

## If tracking has not updated
Wait 2 business days after the ship date. Tracking sometimes shows no change until the first scan. If it still shows no update, or if it shows no movement for several days, contact Customer care. Give your order number, your name, and the delivery address. Staff will contact the carrier.

## Address changes
You can change the address only before the order ships. Contact Customer care at once. Give your order number and the new address. After the order ships, staff cannot change the address, but they may ask the carrier to help. Staff cannot promise this.

## Damage on delivery
Check the machine when it arrives. If the box is damaged, note it on the delivery receipt. Report damage within 7 days. See the Returns and Damaged-on-Arrival Policy.
