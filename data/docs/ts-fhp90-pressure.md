# FH-P90 Troubleshooting: Pressure and Pump Problems

## Safety first
This guide covers pressure and pump problems on the FH-P90 pressure washer. Turn off the engine, release pressure by squeezing the trigger, and engage the trigger lock before you check anything. Never point the wand at people. If a hose bursts, a fitting sprays, or fluid enters the skin, stop using the washer and contact Fernhollow. Get medical help for any injection injury.

## Low pressure
Likely causes and checks, in order:
1. Clogged nozzle. Clean the nozzle hole with the cleaning pin. Try another of the 5 nozzles.
2. Clogged inlet filter. Remove and rinse the water inlet filter.
3. Low water supply. The garden hose must supply enough water. Open the tap fully and check for kinks.
4. Air in the pump. Run water through the pump with the engine off until a steady stream flows.
5. Worn pump seals or a faulty unloader valve. Contact the Service desk.

## Pulsing spray or no water from the wand
Pulsing:
Pulsing usually means air in the pump or a restricted water supply. Check the hose for kinks, clean the inlet filter, and purge air. If pulsing continues, a worn seal or a stuck unloader valve may be the cause.

No water from the wand: check that the tap is on. Check the inlet filter. Check that the nozzle is clicked in fully. Check the high-pressure hose for kinks. Purge air from the pump.

## Water leaking from the pump
A small drip from the pump can mean worn pump seals. A steady leak means seals or a cracked pump head. Stop using the washer and contact the Service desk. Do not keep running a pump that leaks.

## Hose burst or fitting leak
Replace a hose that is cracked or bulged. Check the quick-connect fittings for worn O-rings. A burst hose is a safety risk. Stop using the FH-P90 and contact Fernhollow.

## Pump frozen after winter
If the washer stayed in a cold place with water in the pump, the pump head may be cracked. Signs: water leaks from the pump, or the pump turns hard. Do not start the engine. Freezing damage is not a defect, and the warranty does not cover it. A Fernhollow staff member reviews each case. Next winter, run pump saver through the pump.

## Hose burst, leak, or freezing: when to contact the Service desk
Have the model, the serial number, and photos of the leak ready.
