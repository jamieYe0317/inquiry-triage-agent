# Contact Hours and Channels

## Office hours
Fernhollow Equipment Supply offices are open Monday to Friday, from 8 AM to 5 PM Central Time. The offices are closed on Saturday, Sunday, and national holidays. Messages sent outside these hours are read on the next business day.

## Web form
Use the web form on example.com for all kinds of inquiries. Tell us the model, the serial number, your order number if you have one, and a short description of the problem. Photos help. Staff read each message and reply by email.

## Phone lines
- Orders and Customer care: 555-0100.
- Service desk, for troubleshooting and repairs: 555-0120.
- Partner team, for dealers and distributors: 555-0150.
- Quality team, for defects and safety cases: 555-0160.
Call during office hours. Leave a message if the line is busy, with your name, your number, and the reason for the call.

## Email addresses
- Customer care: care@example.com.
- Service desk: service@example.com.
- Partner team: partners@example.com.
- Quality team: quality@example.com.
Email is not for emergencies. If someone is hurt, call emergency services first.

## Safety cases
If you see fire, smoke, a fuel leak, or any injury with a Fernhollow machine, stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow. For urgent safety cases, call 555-0160 during office hours, or send an email to quality@example.com at any time. Staff read safety emails first.

## Mailing address
Fernhollow Equipment Supply, 100 Example Road, Springfield, IL 00000. Do not mail machines to this address unless staff give you a return authorization in writing.
