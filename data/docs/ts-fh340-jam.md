# FH-340 Troubleshooting: Jams and Clogging

## Safety first
This guide covers jams and clogs on the FH-340 wood chipper. Never clear a jam with your hands. Always do these steps first:
1. Shut off the engine.
2. Pull the spark plug wire.
3. Wait until the drum stops fully.
4. Then clear the hopper or chute with a stick.
If a jam keeps happening or a part is thrown from the machine, stop using the FH-340 and contact Fernhollow.

## Hopper jams and discharge chute clogs
Symptoms: material stops in the hopper, chips do not leave the chute, the chute is packed. Likely causes:
- Wet or leafy material. Wet wood and leaves stick together and pack the chute.
- Feeding too fast. The FH-340 takes branches up to 3 inches. Let the drum clear one branch before you feed the next.
- Dull blades. Dull blades tear wood and make long strings that block the chute.
Check each cause in this order. Clear the chute and check the blades.

## Branches stall the drum and engine bogs down
Symptoms: the drum slows, the engine bogs down, the belt squeals. Likely causes: a branch over 3 inches, feeding too fast, dull blades, or a loose drive belt. Turn off the engine and check the belt tension. Check the blades. Inspect the blades every 25 hours.

## Green or stringy wood wraps around the drum
Green wood and stringy plants, such as vines, wrap around the drum. Shut off the engine, pull the spark plug wire, and wait for the drum to stop. Cut the wrapped material free with a stick and cutters. Never use your hands in the hopper. Let green branches dry a few days if the problem repeats.

## Steps to clear a jam
1. Shut off the engine.
2. Remove the spark plug wire.
3. Wait for full stop.
4. Clear with a stick.
5. Check the chute, the drum, and the blades for damage.
6. Reconnect the wire. Restart.

## Prevention tips
- Feed butt end first.
- Mix leafy material with dry wood.
- Do not feed wet material in a rush.
- Keep the blades sharp or reverse them.
- Check the belt tension every 25 hours.
If the FH-340 still jams with sharp blades and correct belt tension, contact the Fernhollow Service desk with the model and serial number.
