# FH-340 Wood Chipper Owner's Manual

## Specifications
The FH-340 wood chipper is a gas-powered chipper with a belt-driven drum. It has a 7 hp engine. It takes branches up to 3 inches thick. It has two reversible chipper blades. A drive belt moves the drum from the engine. Keep the serial number from the label on the frame for service and warranty questions.

## Setup
Set up the FH-340 wood chipper on flat, level ground outdoors. Attach the hopper, the discharge chute, and the wheels as shown in the parts list. Fill the engine with oil before the first start. Add fresh gasoline. Check that the blade bolts are tight. Check that the drive belt is in place and has the correct tension.

## Safe feeding
Wear safety glasses, hearing protection, gloves, and close-fitting clothes. Never reach into the hopper. Never put your hand, arm, or foot into the hopper or the chute. Feed branches butt end first. Use a stick to push short pieces. Do not feed stones, metal, glass, or plastic. These can throw parts out of the machine and cause injury. Keep bystanders away from the chute. If the machine throws parts, makes a loud knocking noise, or vibrates heavily, stop the engine. Stop using the machine and contact Fernhollow.

## Maintenance
- Inspect the blades every 25 hours of use. Look for dull edges, chips, and cracks.
- Check the belt tension every 25 hours.
- Change the engine oil every 50 hours of use.
- Check that all bolts are tight.
Turn off the engine and pull the spark plug wire before any inspection.

## Blade removal and reversal
1. Turn off the engine and pull the spark plug wire. Wait for the drum to stop.
2. Open the access cover on the FH-340 housing.
3. Remove the blade bolts.
4. Take out the blade. Turn it so the unused edge faces the drum.
5. Fit the blade back. Tighten the bolts to the torque in the parts list.
6. Repeat for the second blade.
7. Close the cover and reconnect the spark plug wire.
After both edges are dull, buy new blades from the parts page. Always replace both blades as a pair.

## Storage
Clear all wood from the hopper and chute. Let the engine cool. For storage longer than 30 days, add fuel stabilizer or drain the tank. Store the FH-340 under a cover in a dry place. For jams, see the FH-340 jams troubleshooting guide. For vibration or belt problems, see the FH-340 vibration guide.
