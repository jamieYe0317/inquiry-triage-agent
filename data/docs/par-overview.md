# Fernhollow Partner Program Overview

## Who can join
The Fernhollow Partner Program is for businesses that sell, rent, or service outdoor power equipment. These include equipment dealers, hardware stores, farm stores, rental companies, and regional distributors. If you want to become a dealer, carry Fernhollow machines in your store, or rent our equipment, this program is the place to start.

## Partner types
There are three partner types:
- Authorized dealer. A store or service business that sells Fernhollow machines and supports customers.
- Rental partner. A rental company that offers Fernhollow machines to customers.
- Regional distributor. A business that supplies dealers in a set of states.
The Partner team decides which type fits each business.

## What partners get
In general terms, partners may get product training, marketing materials, and warranty service authorization. Warranty service authorization lets a partner repair covered machines for Fernhollow customers. The exact items depend on the partner type and the signed agreement.

## Prices and terms
This page lists no prices. Pricing and terms are set in a signed agreement after the Partner team reviews the application. Price lists are shared only after that agreement. Please do not ask for price lists or terms by email before you apply. The Partner team does not give out prices in an inquiry reply.

## How to join
See the guide on how to apply to become a Fernhollow dealer or distributor. You send an online form with your business details. The Partner team reviews it and decides. Joining is not guaranteed. A Fernhollow staff member on the Partner team reviews each application and decides the outcome.
