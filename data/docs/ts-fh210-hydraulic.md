# FH-210 Troubleshooting: Hydraulic Problems

## Safety first
This guide covers hydraulic problems on the FH-210 log splitter. Turn off the engine and let the machine cool before you check anything. Hydraulic fluid under pressure can be hot and can inject under the skin. If fluid sprays out, if a hose bursts, or if you see a large leak, stop using the FH-210 and contact the Fernhollow Service desk.

## Ram moves slowly, stalls on a log, or has weak splitting force
Likely causes and checks, in order:
1. Low fluid. Check the dipstick on a level machine. Add AW-32 hydraulic fluid if it is low.
2. Wrong fluid. The FH-210 needs AW-32 only. A fluid that is too thick or too thin makes the ram slow and weak.
3. Cold fluid. In cold weather, run the engine for 5 minutes with no load.
4. Air in the system. Cycle the ram fully out and in several times with no log.
5. Clogged filter. Check the hydraulic filter. Replace it if it is dirty.
6. Relief valve. If the ram stalls on small logs, the relief valve may be set low or stuck. Do not adjust it yourself. Contact the Service desk.

## Ram will not return
Check the control lever for full travel. Check the fluid level. Check for a bent rod or a log wedged on the wedge. If the ram will not return after these checks, shut off the engine and contact the Service desk.

## Hydraulic fluid leak
Look at the hose fittings, the cylinder rod, and the pump. Loose fittings are the most common cause. With the engine off and the ram at rest, tighten the fitting a little. Do not over-tighten. A leak at the cylinder rod usually means worn cylinder seals. Seals are a repair for Fernhollow. Stop using the FH-210 with a leak and contact the Service desk.

## Whining pump or foamy fluid
A whining pump often means low fluid or air in the system. Foamy or milky fluid means air or water in the fluid. Stop the engine, check the level, and look for a loose suction hose. Drain and replace foamy fluid with fresh AW-32 as the manual states.

## When to stop and contact the Service desk
Contact the Service desk if:
- the leak continues after you tighten the fittings
- the ram still stalls with the correct fluid level
- you see cylinder seal damage or a bent rod
- the pump stays loud after you bleed the air
Have the model, the serial number, and photos ready. A Fernhollow staff member reviews the case. Whether a repair is covered depends on that review.
