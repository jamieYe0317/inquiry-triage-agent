# FH-G7 Troubleshooting: Will Not Start or Shuts Off

## Safety first
This guide covers an FH-G7 portable generator that will not start or shuts off. Use the generator outdoors only, because it makes carbon monoxide. If you see fuel leaking, smoke, or fire, or if you smell fuel strongly, stop using the FH-G7 and contact Fernhollow.

## Engine will not start
Check these items in order:
1. Oil level. The low-oil shutdown sensor stops the engine from starting if oil is low. Add oil to the full mark.
2. Fuel valve. Make sure the fuel valve is open.
3. Fuel. Check the tank has fresh fuel. Old fuel from storage often causes a no-start.
4. Choke position. Use full choke for a cold start. Open it as the engine warms.
5. Spark plug. Pull the plug. If it is wet, black, or dirty, clean or replace it.
6. Ground. The generator must be on level ground. The low-oil sensor can trip on a slope.

## Pull cord is hard to pull
A cord that will not pull can mean the engine has no oil lock, a flooded cylinder, or a problem inside the engine. First, make sure the oil is correct. Remove the spark plug and pull the cord slowly to clear fuel from the cylinder. If the cord stays stuck, stop and contact the Service desk.

## Starts, then shuts off after a few minutes
Likely causes:
- Low oil, which triggers the low-oil shutdown sensor.
- The generator is on a slope.
- A fuel cap vent that is closed.
- Stale fuel or a dirty carburetor.
- A fouled spark plug.
Check the oil level when the engine is off, on level ground. Open the fuel cap vent. Clean the spark plug.

## Engine surges
Surging often comes from stale fuel, a partly blocked carburetor, or a choke left half closed. Open the choke fully once the engine is warm. Use fresh fuel. If surging continues, see the small engine guide for carburetor varnish.

## When to contact the Service desk
Contact the Service desk if the engine does not start after the checks above, if the cord is stuck, or if the engine keeps shutting off with correct oil. Have the model and serial number ready. A Fernhollow staff member reviews the case.
