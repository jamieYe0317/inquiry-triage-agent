# How to Apply to Become a Fernhollow Dealer or Distributor

## Application steps
To become a Fernhollow dealer or distributor, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the online partner application form on example.com.
2. Attach a copy of your business license.
3. Attach your resale certificate.
4. Give the details of your store or service location.
5. Describe your service capability, such as technicians and tools.
6. State your expected annual volume.
7. Give trade references.
Send all items together. An incomplete application takes longer to review.

## What the Partner team looks at
The Partner team reads your business type, your location, your service ability, and your volume. A Fernhollow staff member on the Partner team makes all decisions. An application does not make you a partner. Only a signed agreement does.

## Review timeline
A review takes about 3 to 4 weeks after we receive a complete application. During the review:
- The Partner team may ask more questions by email.
- An interview call is held with applicants who pass the first review.
- A site visit is done for regional distributor applicants.
The timeline can be longer if documents are missing.

## How to check application status
Email the Partner team at partners@example.com or call 555-0150. Give your business name, the date you applied, and the contact name on the form. Staff will tell you the stage of your review. Staff cannot give a decision date in advance.

## Pricing and decisions
Prices are not given during the application. If the Partner team approves the application, pricing and terms are set in a signed agreement. The Partner team makes all decisions. Do not assume approval from an interview call or a site visit.
