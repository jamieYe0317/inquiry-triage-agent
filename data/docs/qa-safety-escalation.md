# Safety Escalation Rule

## What counts as a safety case
Any report of one of these is a safety case:
- fire
- smoke
- burns
- a fuel leak
- electric shock
- parts thrown from a machine
- injury
This applies to every Fernhollow machine: the FH-210 log splitter, the FH-340 wood chipper, the FH-520 stump grinder, the FH-P90 pressure washer, and the FH-G7 generator. It applies if the report comes from a customer, a dealer, or a distributor.

## What the receiving person does
Follow these steps in order:
1. Tell the sender to stop using the machine. Tell them to contact Fernhollow if they have not.
2. If someone is hurt, tell the sender to get medical help first.
3. Collect the model, the serial number, the date, a short description, and photos.
4. Send the case to the Quality team the same day. Mark it urgent.
Do not wait for the full details to send the case.

## What staff do not do in the first reply
Staff do not discuss fault, cause, or compensation in the first reply. Staff do not promise a repair, a refund, or a replacement. Staff do not say the machine is safe to use again. The Quality team reviews the case and decides next steps.

## What the Quality team does
The Quality team contacts the sender, reviews the facts, and may ask for the machine, photos, or service records. A Fernhollow staff member decides the outcome after review. The sender should keep the machine and any damaged parts. Do not repair or throw away the machine until the Quality team says so.
